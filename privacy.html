Privacy & usage

Local first.
Use with care.

These tools are free, privacy-oriented, and designed to process files inside your browser. That reduces exposure, but it does not eliminate every risk.

What stays local

Files opened in a tool are processed by code running on your device. The tools do not intentionally upload file contents, require an account, or send analytics.

Your browser and the site’s hosting provider may still receive ordinary page requests. Following an external link takes you outside this site.

Sensitive data

Diagnostic files can contain passwords, tokens, cookies, personal information, internal addresses, and proprietary data. Review everything before sharing or exporting it. Masking and parsing features may not identify every secret or interpret every file correctly.

Responsible use

Only inspect data and systems you are authorized to access. Keep original files, verify important conclusions independently, and do not treat tool output as security, legal, or professional advice.
